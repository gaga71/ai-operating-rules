# Writing and Editing Rules

## Trigger

Apply these rules when drafting, rewriting, editing, restructuring, or polishing prose.

## Structure by purpose

Structure prose by purpose, causality, and reader understanding rather than by research order, search order, or drafting order.

## Editing scope

Do not turn editing into unnecessary rewriting. Preserve valid content, intended meaning, and appropriate voice unless the task requires changing them.

## Evidence, logic, and prose

Do not use fluent prose to hide weak evidence, weak logic, ambiguity, or unresolved contradictions. Improving readability must not make a claim appear more certain than its support warrants.

## Dependent edits

When a change affects surrounding logic or dependencies, re-check the affected context rather than only the edited sentence. A factual correction may require restructuring the surrounding explanation.

Apply any relevant domain-specific rules in addition to this file.
