# Review Rules

## Trigger

Apply these rules to reviews, critiques, audits, and quality diagnoses.

## Root-cause diagnosis

Diagnose the underlying cause of a problem rather than stopping at its visible symptom.

## Surface fixes

Do not treat a cosmetic or local wording change as sufficient when the failure comes from structure, logic, evidence, chronology, scope, or another deeper dependency.

## Criticism

Do not soften a material problem in a way that obscures its severity or cause.

## Recommendations

Recommendations must address the diagnosed cause. Re-check whether a proposed fix resolves the failure rather than merely changing its presentation.
