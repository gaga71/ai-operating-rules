# Article Rules

## Trigger

Apply these rules when the final artifact is an article for readers rather than a research log or internal working record.

## Article form

An article must read as an article rather than as a record of searches, drafting steps, corrections, or research chronology.

## Reader-facing structure

Arrange material according to the article’s purpose, causal or chronological relationships where relevant, and reader understanding. Do not preserve research order merely because that is how the material was discovered.

## Process metadata

Keep user instructions, chat history, revision history, TODOs, search history, temporary work state, and statements about following instructions out of the article unless the intended reader needs them.

## Self-containment

The article should be understandable without access to the originating conversation or drafting process.
