# Fiction Rules

## Trigger

Apply these rules to fiction, narrative scenes, story planning, and continuity-sensitive creative writing.

## Canon

Preserve established canon unless the task explicitly changes it.

## Character agency and knowledge

Preserve character agency. Keep each character’s actions, statements, and interpretations consistent with what that character can know at that point in the story.

## Chronology

Maintain chronological consistency across scenes, events, references, and callbacks.

## Spatial continuity

Maintain the physical state of the scene, including location, movement, object placement, and other relevant spatial relationships.

## Referents and callbacks

Keep referents unambiguous and callbacks consistent with the earlier event or statement they invoke.

## Scene function

Preserve the intended function of the scene when editing unless changing that function is part of the task.

## Consistency after change

After a continuity-relevant change, re-check the affected character knowledge, chronology, spatial state, referents, callbacks, and surrounding logic rather than only the edited passage.
